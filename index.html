01 — Who I am

About Me

I'm an Information Technology student at Manipal Institute of Technology, currently in the middle of a one-year software development internship at aiRender / Healthstream, where I was selected as one of 11 finalists to work on AI-driven 3D holographic video conferencing.

Most of what I've built started at a hackathon. FraudShield India came out of asking what happens to the 230 million people exposed to UPI scams, and SwaramPay came out of asking what payments look like for someone without a smartphone. Both forced me to learn things properly — event-driven backends, LLM classification across Hindi and Hinglish, speaker-verification models — rather than just well enough for a demo.

Outside coursework I practise DSA in C++ on LeetCode, and I've spent time as a volunteer educator with the Veer Eklavya Foundation, teaching and mentoring school students in Bihar. Before all this I co-authored a paper on cosmic-ray detection using plastic scintillators and SiPMs, which I presented at the Harvard Science Research Conference.
